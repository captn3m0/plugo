> [!CAUTION]
> This was a project to document Plugo's powerbank locations, started
> in March 2022, and lasted till the company's shutdown sometime in Jan 2026.
> Now archived as a historical artifact, you can still see
> the charts tracking the company at <https://captnemo.in/plugo/>.

Where in the world were Plugo's powerbanks.

---

[Plugo](https://plugo.io/) was "India's first smart & fully automated power bank rental service".
They had a mobile app [[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/plugo/id1469215249),
[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.plugo)] that provided
information about where you can find their powerbanks, but the same was not accessible
on the web. This page hosted republished (and slightly cleaned up) data from the Plugo app
, updated on a regular schedule in an easily accessible and searchable format.
